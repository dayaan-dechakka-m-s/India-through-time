Mughal Empire

Babur • Akbar • Shah Jahan • Aurangzeb • Administration

🕌 The Mughal Empire

The Mughal dynasty established political power in northern India after Babur's victory at the First Battle of Panipat in 1526.

Over the following centuries, Mughal rulers expanded their political authority across large parts of South Asia.

Beginning 1526
Important Rulers Babur, Akbar, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb
Major Centres Agra, Delhi, Lahore
Economy Agriculture, revenue and trade

👑 Major Rulers

Babur founded Mughal rule in India.

Akbar expanded the empire and developed important administrative institutions.

Shah Jahan is strongly associated with monumental architecture.

Aurangzeb presided over extensive territorial expansion, especially in the Deccan.

🗺️ Territorial Expansion

01

North India

The early Mughal political base was concentrated in northern India.

02

Central India

Mughal authority expanded into important central regions.

03

Deccan

Later rulers expanded imperial power into the Deccan, although resistance remained significant.

04

Regional Powers

Many local rulers retained different degrees of autonomy or negotiated relationships with the empire.

🏛️ Administration

👑 Emperor

The emperor stood at the centre of the imperial political system.

📊 Mansabdari

The mansabdari system ranked imperial officials and connected status with military and administrative responsibilities.

💰 Jagirdari

Jagirs were revenue assignments given to officials under changing administrative arrangements.

🏢 Provinces

Subahs connected provincial administration to the imperial centre.

🌾 Economy & Agriculture

🌾 Agriculture

Agriculture was the foundation of the imperial economy.

💰 Land Revenue

Land revenue was one of the most important sources of imperial income.

🧵 Textiles

Indian cotton and silk textiles were major commercial products.

🛍️ Markets

Urban markets connected farmers, artisans, merchants and elites.

🚢 Overseas Trade

Indian merchants and European companies participated in expanding Indian Ocean trade.

🎨 Art & Culture

🎨 Painting

Mughal miniature painting developed sophisticated courtly traditions.

📖 Literature

Persian was important at court, while many regional languages also flourished.

🍛 Food

Courtly cuisine developed through interactions among Central Asian, Persian and South Asian traditions.

🌳 Gardens

Formal gardens became an important part of Mughal architectural and political culture.

🧠 Exam Revision

  • Babur established Mughal rule in 1526.
  • Akbar expanded and reorganized the empire.
  • Mansabdari was an important administrative institution.
  • Agriculture and land revenue were central to the economy.
  • Mughal culture combined multiple artistic traditions.
  • Mughal territory changed considerably over time.
