Maurya Empire

Magadha • Pataliputra • Chandragupta • Ashoka

🦁 The Mauryan State

The Maurya Empire emerged from Magadha in the late fourth century BCE and became one of the largest political formations in ancient South Asia.

Its political system combined an imperial centre, provincial administration, officials, military power and relationships with different regional communities.

Beginning c. 322 BCE
Capital Pataliputra
Famous ruler Ashoka
Major evidence Ashokan inscriptions

👑 Important Rulers

Chandragupta Maurya established the dynasty and expanded Mauryan power.

Bindusara continued the expansion of the state.

Ashoka became the most famous Mauryan ruler. His inscriptions provide direct evidence for royal communication and his ideas about dhamma.

🗺️ Territory & Political Control

Mauryan influence extended across large parts of the subcontinent, but control was not necessarily identical everywhere. Core provinces could be more directly administered, while distant regions and frontier zones could have different political relationships.

01

Imperial Core

Magadha and the middle Gangetic region formed the central political base.

02

Provinces

Important regional centres connected distant areas with imperial administration.

03

Frontiers

Frontier areas could have different degrees of integration.

04

Influence

Diplomacy and political relationships extended beyond areas of direct administration.

🏛️ Administration

👑 Emperor

The king stood at the centre of the imperial political structure.

🏢 Officials

Officials helped collect revenue, maintain administration and communicate imperial orders.

🏙️ Provinces

Regional administrative centres helped connect the imperial centre with distant territories.

⚔️ Military

Military organization supported expansion and protection of imperial authority.

💰 Economy

🌾 Agriculture

Agriculture was fundamental to the state and provided much of the revenue base.

💰 Taxation

Revenue collection supported administration, military organization and the imperial court.

🛍️ Markets

Urban centres and markets supported craft production and exchange.

🛣️ Roads

Routes connected political centres and helped movement of people, goods and officials.

🦁 Ashoka & Dhamma

After the Kalinga war, Ashoka's inscriptions emphasized dhamma, ethical behaviour, respect among communities, welfare and responsible conduct.

The inscriptions are among the most important primary sources for understanding Mauryan kingship.

⏳ Timeline

c. 322 BCE

Rise of Chandragupta

Chandragupta Maurya established Mauryan power from Magadha.

c. 3rd BCE

Ashoka

Ashoka ruled a large empire and issued inscriptions across different regions.

After Ashoka

Political Change

Mauryan authority weakened and regional political powers became increasingly important.

🧠 Exam Points

  • Magadha was the political base of Mauryan expansion.
  • Chandragupta Maurya founded the dynasty.
  • Pataliputra was a major imperial centre.
  • Ashoka's inscriptions are major primary sources.
  • Agriculture and taxation supported the state.
  • Mauryan control varied across different regions.
