Maratha History

Shivaji • Forts • Administration • Confederacy

⚔️ Rise of Maratha Power

Maratha political power emerged in the western Deccan during the seventeenth century.

Shivaji built a strong regional state based on forts, military mobility, revenue administration and political organization.

Rise 17th century
Major Figure Shivaji
Core Region Western Deccan
Later Structure Maratha Confederacy

👑 Shivaji

Shivaji established a powerful regional state and used the geography of the Western Ghats to his advantage.

His political system relied on forts, military forces, revenue collection and administrative institutions.

🏛️ The Maratha Confederacy

During the eighteenth century, Maratha power became increasingly decentralized. The Peshwas became powerful, while major families including the Scindias, Holkars, Gaekwads and Bhonsles controlled important territories.

Therefore, the later Maratha political system should not be imagined simply as one tightly centralized empire.
