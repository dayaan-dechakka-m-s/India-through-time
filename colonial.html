✦ COLONIAL SOUTH ASIA ✦

Colonial India

Company Rule • Crown Rule • Economy • Resistance

⚓ Colonial Expansion

British political power in India expanded gradually through warfare, diplomacy, treaties, revenue arrangements and political alliances.

The East India Company became a major political power during the eighteenth century. After the uprising of 1857, governance was transferred to the British Crown in 1858.

Company Era 18th–19th centuries
Crown Rule 1858–1947
Major Turning Point 1857
End 1947

🗺️ Political Structure

British India contained directly administered provinces alongside hundreds of princely states.

Princely rulers retained varying degrees of internal authority under British paramountcy.

💰 Colonial Economy

🌾 Land Revenue

Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari and Mahalwari systems were used in different regions.

🌱 Cash Crops

Commercial cultivation expanded in some regions and periods, depending on market demand.

🧵 Textiles

Indian textile production interacted with changing global markets and British industrial competition.

🚂 Railways

Railways connected markets and regions while also serving administrative, military and commercial purposes.

⚓ Ports

Ports became important nodes in colonial trade networks.

💰 Taxation

Revenue demands were central to colonial administration and affected rural communities.

✊ Resistance

🔥 1857

A major uprising challenged East India Company rule.

🌾 Peasant Movements

Farmers protested against different forms of exploitation and revenue pressure.

🌳 Tribal Resistance

Several tribal communities resisted land alienation, forest restrictions and colonial policies.

🇮🇳 Nationalism

Organized nationalist politics grew strongly during the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries.

📜 1857

The uprising of 1857 involved soldiers, rulers, landholders, peasants and other groups in different regions. Its causes and character varied geographically.

After the rebellion, the British Crown took direct control of governance in 1858.

🧠 Exam Revision

  • British expansion happened gradually.
  • The East India Company was a commercial company that became a political power.
  • Different land revenue systems operated in different regions.
  • Railways and ports transformed movement and markets.
  • The uprising of 1857 ended Company rule.
  • Crown rule began in 1858 and continued until 1947.
