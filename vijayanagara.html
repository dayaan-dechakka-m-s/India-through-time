✦ SOUTH INDIA • HAMPI ✦

Vijayanagara Empire

Hampi • Nayakas • Trade • Temples • Krishnadevaraya

🏛️ The Empire

The Vijayanagara Empire emerged in the fourteenth century and became one of the most powerful states in South India.

Its capital, Vijayanagara, is today represented by the archaeological landscape of Hampi in Karnataka.

Founded 1336 CE
Capital Vijayanagara / Hampi
Famous Ruler Krishnadevaraya
Region South India

👑 Important Rulers

Harihara and Bukka are traditionally associated with the foundation of the empire.

Krishnadevaraya ruled from 1509 to 1529 and is one of the most celebrated Vijayanagara rulers.

🗺️ Territory & Political Control

Vijayanagara's core lay around the Tungabhadra region. At different times the empire exercised authority over large parts of South India through direct administration, provincial authorities, military commanders, tributary relationships and allied rulers.

01

Core

Tungabhadra region and the capital at Vijayanagara.

02

Southern Provinces

Different regions were administered through local and military authorities.

03

Nayaka Territories

Nayaka authorities controlled territories while remaining connected to the imperial system.

04

Influence

Diplomacy, warfare and trade extended Vijayanagara's regional influence.

🌾 Economy

🌾 Agriculture

Agriculture was a major foundation of state revenue and urban prosperity.

💧 Water Management

Tanks, channels and reservoirs supported farming and urban settlement.

🛍️ Markets

Hampi contained important commercial areas associated with markets and specialized goods.

🐎 Horse Trade

Imported war horses were strategically important for elite cavalry forces.

🧵 Textiles

Textiles formed part of regional and long-distance trade.

💎 Precious Goods

Precious stones and luxury goods were associated with elite markets and trade.

🛕 Hampi

The Hampi landscape contains temples, bazaars, royal structures, gateways, water systems and monumental architecture. It provides historians and archaeologists with evidence about political, religious and economic life.

⚔️ Warfare

Vijayanagara competed with several Deccan sultanates and other regional powers. Warfare affected territory, trade routes and political alliances.

The Battle of Talikota in 1565 was a major turning point. The capital suffered severe destruction, although Vijayanagara political traditions continued afterward.
